Example datasets#

We tried to identify two smaller datasets from microbes and are able to present two ecoli-datasets obtained from a bigger collection of E. coli projects. Thanks to Caroline Jachmann for the great work assembling a peptide atlas with post translational modifications for ecoli:

  • E. coli peptide atlas with phosphosites

We consider the following datasets:

  • PXD040621 - broccoli-derived antioxidant sulforaphane in human gut - article

  • PXD041301 - membrane proteins in a time-course experiment - article
